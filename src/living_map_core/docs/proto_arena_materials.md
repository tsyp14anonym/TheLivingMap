# Prototype arena materials

- Wall length to build (after the door openings): **37.5 m**, height 0.61 m
- Plywood 12 mm, standard sheet 1.22 x 2.44 m cut lengthwise into two 0.61 x 2.44 m strips: **8 sheets** (16 strips) + 10 % spare = **9 sheets**
- Each strip = one 2.44 m wall (or two 1.22 m modules); strips are joined with 2 right-angle brackets + clamps so the layout can be changed in minutes
- Floor: leave the bare floor (smooth) or lay 6 mm MDF/hardboard; the yard outside the entrance is just tape on the floor
- Rubble: 6-10 pieces of foam/cardboard blocks, 10-15 cm high, stacked across the south-east door (impassable for the ground robot)
- Obstacles: 4 cardboard boxes 0.4 x 0.4 m
